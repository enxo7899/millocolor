# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mixed audience, professionals first: auto body shops and refinish painters, industrial and furniture finishers, plus the wider public (car owners, workshops, small buyers) in Albania and Kosovo. They arrive to find the right paint, abrasive or spray equipment, to get colour-matching or training help, or to locate a shop. Site is bilingual (English, Albanian).

## Product Purpose

Millocolor sh.p.k. is an Albanian distributor of paints, coatings, materials and equipment for automotive refinishing, industrial painting and furniture finishing, established 1993. The site must show credibility (authorised dealer of established brands, long history, real premises) and move a visitor to call, WhatsApp, or visit a branch. There is no online checkout.

## Positioning

An expert local distributor, not a webshop: multi-brand range plus in-house colour matching/mixing lab, technical training, and equipment support, delivered from four physical locations (Tirana HQ, Tirana branch, Elbasan, Pristina) since 1993.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual EN/SQ via next-intl; Next.js 15 App Router, Tailwind, framer-motion, GSAP, react-three-fiber.
- Primary conversion: call, WhatsApp, email, or visit (contact details in `data/contact.ts`).
- Product catalogue and brand list exist in `data/`; services in `data/services.ts`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Millocolor / MILLOCOLOR. Logo wordmark is a heavy rounded sans with MILLO in blue and COLOR in red, as on the storefront sign (blue about #2f50bd, red about #c9404b).
- Personality: precise, expert, trustworthy; premium but not flashy.
- Owner wants the spray-gun-painting-the-name idea kept in the hero.
- Owner dislikes anything that reads as amateur or generic AI template.

## Evidence on Hand

Real assets: four building photos (`public/images/buildings`), 26 product photos (`public/images/products`), 16 partner logos (`public/images/partners`, `normalized/`), storefront sign photo (shared in chat, not in repo), spray gun 3D model (`public/models`), a dark car video (`public/videos/hero.mp4`). `public/images/aston-martin-f1-hero-bg.jpg` carries third-party sponsor branding and must not be used. No testimonials, customer names, prices or statistics beyond "since 1993" and the four locations exist; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Earn trust with specifics (brands, places, years, services), never with claims.
- Make the next step (call, WhatsApp, visit) always one tap away.
- Real photography and real products over decoration.
- Same quality in Albanian as in English.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard access, reduced-motion respected; mobile-first reality for shop owners on phones.
